                DOCUMENT PREPARATION

                Documents to Keep Ready

                • Aadhaar card and Aadhaar-linked bank details, as required.
                • Valid caste certificate and caste validity, where applicable.
                • Current-year income certificate, if required by the scheme.
                • Previous examination marksheets.
                • Admission receipt, bonafide certificate and fee receipt.
                • CAP allotment letter, if applicable.
                • Domicile certificate and other documents requested by the portal.

                The exact list differs by scheme and student. Confirm the current document requirements with your college scholarship section.
